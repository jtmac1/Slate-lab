Slate Lab Grabber

Does what Pull ETR + Blick and the ETR reports read do, with no Claude. You click it on each page yourself; it saves a file to Downloads and the hub files it on Refresh (or drop the file on the hub).

1. Install (once)

Show the bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B), then drag this button onto it:

⤓ Slate Lab grab

2. Use it on Sunday

ETR projectionsOpen ETR's DraftKings projections page (or the showdown, or Early/Afternoon page), wait for the table, click the button.
Blick ownershipOpen Blick's GPP ownership page (classic or showdown), click.
Blick conditionalOpen Blick's conditional ownership page, wait a few seconds, click. Feeds the Lab stacks.
ETR data tablesOpen the DvP, Expected Fantasy Points or Pass Rate Over Expectation page, click.
ETR articlesOpen any article (Top Plays, GPP Leverage, Silva's Matchups...), click. Saved word for word for the Brain and marked read on the Notes tab.
ShowsOn YouTube (the Million, Establish The Show), open the transcript ("...more", then "Show transcript"), click.
ThenPress Refresh in the hub. Type your own player calls in the Notes tab's reads box, and pick Lab rules in the Simulator's Brain menu if you're going without Claude.

Without Claude, articles are saved as text but not turned into the slate guide's likes and fades; that's what your reads box is for.
